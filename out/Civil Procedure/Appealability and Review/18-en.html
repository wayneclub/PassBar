A notice of appeal 上诉通知书 (Notice of Appeal) A formal document filed by a party to initiate an appeal in a higher court. must be filed with the district court clerk 地区法院书记官 (District Court Clerk) The official responsible for maintaining court records and receiving filings. within 30 days after the district court enters final judgment 录入终审判决 (Entry of Final Judgment) The formal entry of the court's final decision on the docket, starting the appeal clock. to allow an appellate court 上诉法院 (Appellate Court) A court having jurisdiction to review decisions of a trial-level or lower court. to acquire jurisdiction over the case.* But if a party files a posttrial motion 审后动议 (Posttrial Motion) A motion filed after trial (e.g., JMOL, new trial) requesting the court to alter its judgment. within 28 days after the entry of final judgment, then the notice of appeal need not be filed until 30 days after the court enters an order disposing of that motion.

Here, the district court entered a final judgment against the man on March 1 . The man timely filed a renewed motion for judgment as a matter of law (JMOL) within 28 days after entry of final judgment—on March 21 . The judge denied this motion on April 15 . The man then timely filed a notice of appeal with the clerk of the district court within 30 days after the denial—on May 1 . Therefore, the appellate court has jurisdiction to hear this appeal (Choice B).

*The time to file the notice of appeal is extended to 60 days if one of the parties is the United States, a federal agency, or a federal officer or employee sued in an official capacity.

(Choice A) A notice of appeal must be filed with the clerk of the district court, not the appellate court.

(Choice C) A party can file a renewed motion for JMOL 法律判决动议 (JMOL Motion) Judgment as a Matter of Law; requested when a party believes no reasonable jury could find otherwise. within 28 days after the entry of final judgment only if the party moved for JMOL before the case was submitted to the jury (as seen here). However, the fact that the man preserved the renewed motion for JMOL does not grant the appellate court jurisdiction.

Educational Objective

A notice of appeal must be filed with the district court clerk within 30 days after the entry of final judgment unless a posttrial motion is filed within 28 days after final judgment is entered. In that case, the notice of appeal must be filed within 30 days after the court enters an order disposing of the motion.
