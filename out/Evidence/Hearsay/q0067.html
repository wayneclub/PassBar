Statements in learned treatises hearsay exception (FRE 803(18))

FRE = Federal Rules of Evidence

An out-of-court statement (eg, a passage from a book) is barred by the rule against hearsay传闻证据规则：法庭原则上禁止引入庭外陈述来证明其所主张内容的真实性。 when offered to prove the truth of the matter asserted therein. However, statements in a learned treatise学术论著中的陈述：作为传闻证据规则的例外，在特定条件下可采信。, periodical, or pamphlet are excepted from this rule and are admissible for their truth when:

  • the publication is established as a reasonably reliable authority可靠的权威：指出版物被认可为该领域内可靠的学术或专业标准。 by a party's expert or judicial notice司法认知：法官无需举证直接认定无可争议的事实。 and
  • the statements are called to the attention of or relied on by an expert witness专家证人：在特定领域具有专业知识并在法庭上发表意见的证人。 during examination.

Expert testimony that a treatise is a reliable authority (as seen here) lays a foundation for admitting the statements therein. The statements can then be used as a basis for the expert's opinion and as substantive evidence实质性证据：直接用于证明案件事实真相的证据，而非仅用于弹劾证人。 to help prove a material fact (here, the proper standard of psychiatric care) (Choice D). However, the expert may only read the statements into evidence—the treatise itself cannot be received as an exhibit法庭展品/物证：正式提交给陪审团查看并作为法庭记录的实物或文件。 (Choice B).

(Choice A) It is true that a witness's credibility可信度/可信性：证人陈述是否值得信任的属性。 cannot be supported unless attacked. But here, the expert seeks to read the passages to establish the proper standard of psychiatric care—not to support his credibility.

📌 Educational Objective

Statements in a learned treatise, periodical, or pamphlet are excepted from the rule against hearsay when (1) the publication is established as a reasonably reliable authority by a party's expert or judicial notice and (2) the statements are called to the attention of or relied on by an expert witness during examination.
