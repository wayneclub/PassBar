The rule against hearsay bars the admission of an out-of-court statement offered to prove the truth of the matter asserted therein. When such a statement (eg, the neighbor's threat) is contained within another (eg, the husband's description of the encounter), neither statement is admissible unless both are excluded or exceptedExcluded or Excepted / 排除或例外Exclusions define statements as nonhearsay (FRE 801(d)); exceptions allow hearsay statements to be admitted due to reliability (FRE 803/804). / 排除（非传闻）与例外（符合特定可靠性条件的传闻证据）。 from the rule against hearsay.

One hearsay exclusionHearsay Exclusion / 传闻排除An out-of-court statement that is defined as nonhearsay by FRE 801(d) (eg, an opposing party's statement). / 联邦证据规则 801(d) 规定的非传闻情形（如当事人对手的陈述）。 applies to statements by a party-opponent that are offered against that party. Since the neighbor is a party to this lawsuit and his statement is being offered against him, that statement is excluded from hearsay (ie, constitutes nonhearsay). But since the husband's statement describing his chance meeting with the neighbor does not fall within any hearsay exclusion or exceptionHearsay Exception / 传闻例外An out-of-court statement that, though hearsay, is admissible under FRE 803 or 804 (eg, excited utterance, state of mind). / 即使是传闻也因符合特定可信条件（如兴奋宣泄、当时心境等）而可以被采信。,* the woman's testimony about both statements is inadmissible (Choice C).

*The hearsay exception for statements offered against a party that wrongfully caused the declarant's unavailability does not apply here because the neighbor did not shoot the husband with the intent to prevent him from testifying.

(Choice B) Evidence of a prior bad act cannot be used to prove a person's propensity to behave in a certain manner. But here, the neighbor's statement was offered to prove that he killed the husband—not that the neighbor was prone to violence. Therefore, this rule is inapplicable.

(Choice D) The hearsay exception for statements of a declarant's then-existing state of mindThen-Existing State of Mind / 当时心境例外A hearsay exception for statements showing the declarant's then-existing mental, emotional, or physical condition under FRE 803(3). / 联邦证据规则 803(3) 传闻例外，涉及声明者当时存在的精神、情绪或身体状况。 is unnecessary because the neighbor's statement is nonhearsay. And even if this exception applied, the neighbor's statement would remain inadmissible because it is contained within the husband's inadmissible hearsay statement.

📌 Educational Objective

When an out-of-court statement that is offered for the truth of the matter asserted therein is contained within another, neither statement is admissible unless both are excepted or excluded from the rule against hearsay.
