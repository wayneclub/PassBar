Under the rule against hearsay传闻证据 (Hearsay): 指陈述者在法庭外作出的陈述，在法庭上被引入作为证据以证明该陈述内容的真实性。一般不可采信，除非符合例外情形。, an out-of-court statement法庭外陈述 (Out-of-Court Statement): 指陈述者在审判或听证会之外作出的陈述。若用于证明所陈述事项的真实性，通常属于传闻证据。 (first statement to newspaper) is generally inadmissible to prove the truth of the matter asserted therein (description of the accident). However, such statements may be admissible for nonhearsay purposes, such as impeaching a witness弹劾证人 (Impeaching a Witness): 通过展示证人不诚实、偏见、先前不一致陈述或感知缺陷，来削弱其证言的可信度。. One way a witness can be impeached is by presenting evidence of his/her prior inconsistent statement先前不一致陈述 (Prior Inconsistent Statement): 证人过去作出的与法庭证言不一致的法庭外陈述。通常仅用于弹劾，不能用于证明实体事实，除非该陈述是在宣誓宣判下作出的。, as the estate did here. Therefore, the court should overrule (not sustain) the estate's hearsay objection (Choice D).

However, when evidence is admissible for one purpose but not another—e.g., for impeachment but not for its truth—the adverse party may request that the court limit the evidence to its proper scope and instruct the jury accordingly. If that request is timely,* then the court must then give the limiting instruction限制性指示 (Limiting Instruction): 法官对陪审团作出的指示，要求其仅能为了特定目的或针对特定当事人来考虑某项证据。 (Choice A).

Additionally, under the rule of completeness完整性规则 (Rule of Completeness): 联邦证据规则第106条。当一方引入书面或录音陈述的一部分时，对方可以要求同时引入其他部分或另一陈述，以确保公平和避免误导。, an adverse party may introduce any other part of the admitted statement OR any other statement that should, in fairness, be considered with the admitted statement. This rule seeks to remedy the misleading effects that an out-of-context or incomplete statement may have on a fact finder. Therefore, the witness's follow-up statement to the newspaper should be admitted because it completes the witness's earlier statement about the accident (Choice B).

*Although there is no strict rule on the timing of a request for a limiting instruction, a request made immediately after the introduction of the contested evidence is always deemed timely.

📌 Educational Objective

When evidence is admissible for one purpose but not another, that evidence can be limited to its proper scope and the jury can be instructed accordingly upon a timely request. Any other part of the admitted statement OR any other statement that should be considered alongside the admitted statement may also be introduced.
