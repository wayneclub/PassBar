Hearings on preliminary questions
(FRE 104)
Preliminary question must be heard outside jury's presence if:
  • hearing is on admissibility of confession
  • criminal defendant-witness so requests or
  • justice so requires
FRE = Federal Rule of Evidence

Under Federal Rule of Evidence 104, the court must decide preliminary questions on whether evidence is admissible, a privilege exists, or a witness is qualified to testify. Hearings regarding such determinations must be conducted outside the presence of the jury if:

  • the hearing involves the admissibility of a confession
  • a defendant in a criminal case is a witness and so requests or
  • justice so requires.

Here, whether the defendant was Mirandized米兰达警告 (Mirandize)：在羁押审讯前向被告宣告的宪法权利（如沉默权、聘请律师权等）。 prior to a custodial interrogation羁押性审讯 (Custodial Interrogation)：嫌疑人在被剥夺自由的情况下受到的审问，会触发米兰达警告的要求。 is a preliminary question that the court must decide to ultimately rule on the admissibility of the defendant's confession. Since the hearing involves the admissibility of a confession, it must be conducted outside the presence of the jury. As a result, the court should grant the defendant's request (Choice D).

(Choice B) The defendant's confession is a statement by a party-opponent对方当事人的陈述 (Statement by a Party-Opponent)：联邦证据规则下被视为非传闻（Non-hearsay），可直接用作不利于该当事人的证据。 and is therefore nonhearsay when offered against the defendant. But the confession would still be inadmissible under the Fifth Amendment第五修正案 (Fifth Amendment)：包含反对强迫自证其罪的条款，是米兰达规则的宪法基础。 if the defendant was not properly Mirandized before making the confession. Therefore, this is not a basis to deny the defendant's request for a hearing and admit the confession.

(Choice C) Both written (signed or unsigned) and oral confessions are admissible in criminal cases.

📌 Educational Objective

Hearings on preliminary questions must be held outside the jury's presence if (1) the hearing involves the admissibility of a confession, (2) a defendant in a criminal case is a witness and so requests, or (3) justice so requires.
