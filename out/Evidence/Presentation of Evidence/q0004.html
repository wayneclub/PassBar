Impeachment v. Substantive use

Impeachment
Question establishes bias, self-interest, etc.
Dual
purpose
Substantive use
Question goes toward material fact or issue

Under Federal Rule of Evidence 607, a witness can be impeached弹劾 (Impeach): 挑战或削弱证人证言的可信度 (credibility)。 by any party—including the party that called the witness. One method of impeachment is establishing that the witness is biased偏见 (Bias): 证人因亲属关系、雇佣关系、敌意或个人利益而对某一方当事人产生倾向性，可作为弹劾的根据。 against a party and therefore had a motive to lie说谎动机 (Motive to lie): 证人在作证时因为特定利益、过节或背景而产生说谎的动机，属偏见弹劾的一部分。 while testifying. For example, evidence that the supervisor learned that the plaintiff had reported her to the police before the firing shows that the supervisor may have reason to lie about why the plaintiff was fired. Therefore, the plaintiff's question is permissible for impeachment purposes (Choice A).

But a question that impeaches a witness may also be used to elicit substantive evidence实质性证据 (Substantive evidence): 用于证明案件中实体事实或争议焦点的证据，而不仅仅是用来弹劾证人。 if the question goes toward a material fact or issue—ie, a fact or issue that is of consequence in determining the outcome of the case. Here, the plaintiff sued for wrongful discharge, so the reason he was fired is a material fact. Since the plaintiff's question tends to establish an improper motive不当动机 (Improper motive): 指解雇或惩罚原告时的报复、歧视等非法动机，直接关系到实体争议。 for discharging him, it is also permissible for this substantive purpose (Choice C).

(Choice B) A party may impeach a witness by inquiring into specific instances of conduct that bear on the witness's character for truthfulness诚实品格 (Character for truthfulness): 证人在诚实守信方面的个人品行特征，通常用于弹劾或支持其可信度。. But here, the plaintiff's question addressed the supervisor's response to learning that the plaintiff reported her to the police—not whether the supervisor actually pilfered the office coffee fund. Therefore, the question does not go to the supervisor's character for truthfulness.

📌 Educational Objective

A witness may be impeached by showing that the witness is biased against a party and therefore had a motive to lie while testifying. A question that impeaches a witness may also be used to elicit substantive evidence if the question concerns a material fact or issue.
