Use of witness's prior inconsistent statement

Impeachment
purposes When statement is inconsistent with current testimony
Dual
purposes
Substantive

A prior inconsistent statementPrior inconsistent statement (先前矛盾陈述): 证人此前做出的与当庭证言不一致的陈述，主要用于弹劾证人可信度。 is a statement that was made by a witness in the past that is inconsistent with the witness's current testimony. The prior statement may be used to impeachImpeachment (弹劾/质疑): 在交叉询问中引入证据以削弱或攻击证人可信度的行为。 (i.e., discredit) the witness by:

  • examining the witness about the statement (i.e., intrinsic evidenceIntrinsic evidence (内部证据): 在交叉询问中通过询问证人本身所揭示的证据，不需要引入外部证人或文件。) or
  • introducing the statement through extrinsic evidenceExtrinsic evidence (外部证据): 在交叉询问之外，通过引入其他证人证言或书面文件来证明某一事实的证据。 if (1) the witness first has an opportunity to explain or deny, and the opposing party can question the witness about, the statement or (2) justice so requires.

But a prior inconsistent statement may not be used as substantive evidenceSubstantive evidence (实质性证据): 用以证明案件争议事实本身真实性的证据，而非仅用于弹劾证人的可信度。—i.e., to prove the truth of the matter asserted—unless the statement is excludedHearsay exclusion (传闻排除): 联邦证据规则中明确规定不属于传闻的陈述，例如表态证人的先前陈述或对方当事人的陈述。 or exceptedHearsay exception (传闻例外): 虽属传闻但在法律规定下可予采信的例外情形。 from the rule against hearsayHearsay (传闻证据): 证人在法庭审理之外做出的，被引入用以证明其所断言事项真实性的陈述，通常不可采。.

Here, the woman testified that she saw the man burglarize the warehouse. The man's attorney then called the woman's cellmate to testify that the woman had previously said that she broke into the warehouse alone. And because the woman was given the opportunity explain or deny—and the prosecution can question her about—her prior inconsistent statement, the cellmate's testimony is admissible for impeachment purposesImpeachment (弹劾目的): 仅用于质疑证人诚实度或可信度，不能用于证明陈述内容的真实性。. But the woman's prior statement cannot be used substantively because it is neither excluded* nor excepted from the hearsay rule.

*The hearsay exclusion for statements by a declarant-witness does not apply because the woman's prior inconsistent statement was neither made under penalty of perjury at a legal proceeding nor identified a person as someone the declarant perceived earlier.

(Choice A) A prior inconsistent statement is not hearsayHearsay (传闻证据): 证人在法庭审理之外做出的，被引入用以证明其所断言事项真实性的陈述。 when it is offered for impeachment and not for the truth of the matter asserted.

(Choice B) Prior inconsistent statements need not be clearly corroborated (i.e., supported by other evidence) to be admissible.

(Choice C) A statement against penal interestStatement against penal interest (不利于刑事利益的陈述): 传闻证据的例外之一。指陈述人做出的一旦公开会使其面临刑事指控的陈述，仅在陈述人“无法出庭”时适用。 is a hearsay exception for out-of-court statements that tend to expose the declarant to criminal liability. But since this exception only applies when the declarant (e.g., the woman) is unavailable as a witness, it does not apply here.

📌 Educational Objective

Extrinsic evidence of a prior inconsistent statement is admissible for impeachment if (1) the witness first has a chance to explain or deny, and the opposing party can question the witness about, that statement or (2) justice so requires. And a prior statement is admissible for substantive purposes if it falls within a hearsay exclusion or exception.
