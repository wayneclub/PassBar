Expert witnessExpert witness (专家证人): 凭借科学、技术或其他专业知识，在法庭上对专门问题提供意见的证人。 Admissible from qualified* expert who:
  • testifies based on sufficient facts or data acquired by reliable principles & methods
  • has reasonable degree of certainty in opinion and
  • helps trier of fact understand evidence or fact issue
*Has knowledge, skill, experience, training, or education in substantive area.

A witness may testify at trial to any matter:

  • that is relevantRelevant evidence (相关证据): 具有使任何对诉讼结果有影响的事实发生概率增加或减少的倾向性。—ie, tends to make a material fact more or less probable and
  • about which that witness has personal knowledgePersonal knowledge (亲身认知): 证人必须对所作证的事项有第一手的直接感知或观察。—ie, knowledge based on firsthand observation or experience.

Here, the man sought to testify about the condition of the brake shoe that allegedly caused the accident (relevanceRelevance (相关性): 证据与案件事实之间的关联，是证据可采性的首要前提。). He had observed the condition of the brake shoe firsthand since he had exhibited it and pointed out the alleged defect to the mechanic's expert (personal knowledgePersonal knowledge (亲身认知): 对事物的真实状态拥有直接的第一手经验。). Therefore, the man's testimony is admissible.

📌 Educational Objective

Witnesses can testify to any relevant matter about which they have personal knowledge—ie, knowledge based on a firsthand observation or experience.
