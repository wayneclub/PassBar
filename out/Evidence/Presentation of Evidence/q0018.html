FRE = Federal Rules of Evidence

A witness's character for truthfulnessCharacter for Truthfulness (诚实品质): A witness's general trait for honesty or dishonesty, which is key to determining their credibility. (ie, credibility) may be impeachedImpeachment (弹劾/质疑可信度): The process of challenging the credibility of a witness at trial to show they are not worthy of belief. through any of the following methods:

  • Reputation testimonyReputation Testimony (名誉证言): Testimony by a witness about what is generally said about another person's character in the community or among associates. – testimony by someone sufficiently familiar with the witness's reputation among associates or in the community
  • Opinion testimonyOpinion Testimony (意见证言): Testimony expressing the witness's personal belief or assessment regarding another person's character. – testimony sharing an opinion on the witness's character that is based on personal knowledge and familiarity with the witness
  • Specific instances of conduct (SICs) that are probative of the witness's character for truthfulness

SICs can always be introduced intrinsicallyIntrinsic Evidence (内部证据): Evidence elicited directly from the witness being cross-examined, without introducing outside evidence or calling other witnesses. (ie, by questioning the witness being impeached about the SIC). But they can only be introduced extrinsicallyExtrinsic Evidence (外部证据): Evidence from other sources (such as other witnesses or documents) introduced to impeach or prove a fact, rather than just the testifying witness's testimony. (ie, from other sources) if the SIC relates to a criminal conviction for a felony or crime of dishonesty.

Here, the plaintiff's counsel seeks to impeach the defendant's witness by cross-examining the witness about falsifying an affidavit to escape paying sales tax. This SIC bears on the witness's credibility, and it is being introduced through the witness's own testimony (ie, intrinsically). Therefore, the question is proper.

(Choice A) Character can be proved by SICs when character is an essential elementEssential Element (必要要素): A character trait that is a core part of a claim, charge, or defense (e.g., character in a defamation or child custody case). of a charge, claim, or defense OR on cross-examination when the SIC is probative of the witness's character for truthfulness.

(Choice B) Any witness can be impeached, including those who are impeaching another witness.

(Choice D) Reputation or opinion testimony can be challenged by questioning the witness's standard of judgment—eg, "You claim she has an untruthful reputation, but did you know she was voted Citizen of the Year?" But here, the question goes to the defense witness's own conduct—not the reputation of the plaintiff's witness.

📌 Educational Objective

A witness may be impeached by a specific instance of conduct that is probative of the witness's character for truthfulness. But this conduct must be introduced with intrinsic evidence unless it is a conviction for a felony or crime of dishonesty.
